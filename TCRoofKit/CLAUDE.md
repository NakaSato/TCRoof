# `TCRoofKit` Swift package

Read with the root CLAUDE.md (build, test and data rules). Run package commands from this folder.

**Local dependencies:** `../GrowattAPI` (`.package(path:)`), Growatt's OpenAPI and web portal clients, which `TCRoofCore` depends on and re-exports (`@_exported import GrowattAPI` in `TCRoofCore/GrowattAPI+Overview.swift`): code here uses `GrowattAPI` and `GrowattPortal` without importing them. Its own rules and tests: `GrowattAPI/CLAUDE.md`. A change there rebuilds this package; run both packages' tests (`swift test` in each).

**`TCRoofKit` Swift package** holds all survey work, layered as follows:
- `TCRoofUI`: all the package's UI. Its root files and `Gallery/` are the design system shared by the app and the survey module (`docs/ui/components`), and `Survey/` is the survey editor (below). `Theme` tokens (light and dark), `Space` / `Radius` / `TypeScale`, `Font.sarabun`, and `TC*` components by board section, plus the DEBUG `ComponentGallery`. The design system files import nothing from the other modules; keep it so. New screens build from these components; colours come from `Theme`, never hex literals in views. No `Color.white` either: cards and fields use `Theme.surface`, text on `Theme.primary` uses `Theme.onPrimary`, and plans and diagrams sit on `Theme.paper` (white in both modes, since their ink is dark). Buttons use `TCButtonStyle`, not `.bordered` / `.borderedProminent`: a page's `.foregroundStyle` overrides the system style's white label. The app is light only: it forces light mode for signed-in users (`TCRoofApp.swift`). `Theme` keeps its dark values, but dark mode is not shown.
- `TCRoofCore`: pure values and rules, no UI.
  - `PWAOverview.swift`: `InstallStationSummary` / `InstallOverview` and `MaintenanceStationSummary` / `MaintenanceOverview`, the iPhone survey home's `งานติดตั้ง` and `บำรุงรักษา` overviews (behind / ahead of the MP plan, kWp-weighted averages). The app fills them through `PWAOverviewHost` (`\.pwaOverview`), since MP plans and daily reports are app models.
  - `PWAPlan.swift`: the iPhone PWA `แผนงาน` page (`docs/ui/pwa-plan-iphone`): the day agenda, the kWp-weighted S-curve, the Gantt bars and the deadlines of two weeks, from `PWAPlan.Station`s the app makes from its jobs (`Job.planStation`).
  - `MasterSchedule+Links.swift`: the MP plan's links (docs/specs/master-schedule-swift.md §5). `PermitLink` books 1.4 / 1.5 / 1.7 at 100 % on the approval day once the station's site papers (or the ERC flow's steps 6 / 11) approve them; `Job.stationDocs`'s setter runs it, and so do ตั้งค่าแผน, the workbook import and the assistant's re-plan after saving the plan (`Job.bookPermitsFromDocs`), once per task (`Actual.fromDocuments`), never lowering anything. `IECReport.missingPhotoLabels` names a construction task's missing `9 ภาพถ่ายติดตั้งระบบ` photos; booking that task to 100 % (progress sheet, assistant card, daily report sign) asks first, and `บันทึกทั้งที่ยังไม่มีรูป` goes on.
  - `Models.swift`: `Job`, `SurveyModule`, `Values` / `FieldValue`, `Transformer` / `Board` / `Breaker`.
  - `Schema.swift`: every page and section as `ModuleSpec` / `GroupSpec` / `FieldSpec` with field ids.
  - `Progress.swift`: completion, section numbering (`sectionsByCategory`: B1, B1.2 …), `steps`.
  - `IECReport.swift`: a station's IEC 62446 test before and after the grid connection (`docs/specs/iec62446-excel-export-swift.md` §2): per inverter 20 string slots in Excel order, the checks, 7-day energy and test conditions, with derived kWp, Yf, PR, efficiency, the IR summary, the Voc check and progress. Stored as `Job.iecReportJSON`; `IECReport(form:)` reads the form JSON of `docs/forms/iec62446-pwa`. `IECReport+Stages` has the 7 steps of the wizard (done and missing per step, derived), their confirmations (kept with a digest of the step's values, so a later edit unconfirms), the exports, the hand-over and closing result (the contractor's `ส่งผลให้ทีม` locks the test, the internal team's `ปิดการทดสอบ` closes it and books MP 3.1, or `ส่งกลับให้แก้` returns it; `Job.updateIEC` refuses edits while it's sent or closed, until `แก้ไข`) and the photos' Drive names (`IEC 62446/รูปถ่าย`, uploaded with the job's documents). `IECReport+Install` is `9 ภาพถ่ายติดตั้งระบบ`, the hand-over's installation photo report: its pages follow the station's roofs (`installPhotos.roofs`, two pages each) and inverters (whose two photos are step 1's), then ตู้ AC, ตู้ DC, Smart energy, the grounds and PQM; the station grid's `ภาพถ่ายติดตั้งระบบ` item takes them (`IECInstallPhotosPage`; on iPhone, one column, from the PWA job screen's `ภาพถ่ายติดตั้งระบบ` row, `IECInstallPhotosScreen`) and `PDF รายงาน 9` draws the cover, its contents and every page (`PDFReport/InstallPhotoPages`, the design canvas's `IECInstallCover` / `IECInstall1–12`) into the job's `IEC 62446` documents and, made by the team on a real station, as the station paper `install_photo_report` (status `ร่างแล้ว` when it had none), so the Drive upload files it in the hand-over folders.
  - `SiteICL.swift`: the PWA site ICL (ICL ตรวจหน้างาน, 69 items in 10 groups from the กปภ. standard drawings and IEC 62446, `Resources/icl-site-items.json` = `docs/specs/master-plan/icl-site-items.json`, written from the Master Plan's `icl_items.py`; `SiteICLTests` keeps them the same). A sheet per group, group 2.1 one per installation point (its mounting picks its items); the contractor fills and sends a sheet (`icl.site_fill`), the team signs it or sends it back (`icl.site_sign`). The app keeps each sheet as an `ICLSheet` (one `iclSheets` document each, `state` beside the JSON for firestore.rules, `SiteICL.Sheet.trusting` drops a signature the state doesn't record) with its photos (`PhotoAsset.iclSheet`, tag `sheet#item`, uploaded to `ICL หน้างาน/`).
  - `ProjectFeed.swift`: `FeedPost` and `FeedBuilder`, the iPhone home's project feed made from daily reports, ICL check lists and punch lists (`docs/ui/tcroof-home-iphone-v3`).
  - `ProjectRisk.swift`: the Master Plan's project risk register (`RiskRegister`, CHA-81), read only from `config/risks`, which the team's Google Sheet writes (`tools/master-plan-sheet`); the app's `RiskRegisterStore` keeps it, team only.
  - `Stakeholders.swift`: the Master Plan's stakeholder list (`StakeholderList`, CHA-82), read only from `config/stakeholders`, which the same sheet writes; each row's `ความถี่` / `กำหนดส่ง` give its cadence (weekly on a weekday, monthly on a day, or days before each station's handover) and `dues` the reports due over the next days. They join the plan page's `กำหนดส่ง` (kind `report`, `PWAPlan.stakeholderDeadlines`), the routine-report reminders at 08:00 the day before (`StakeholderNotification`) and the assistant's look-ahead deadlines; the app's `StakeholderStore` keeps it, team only.
  - `SystemNotifications.swift` and `LiveNotifications.swift`: every local notification as a `NotificationPlan` (survey, sync, Drive, routine reports, calibration, install days, the MP plan and its leading indicators, `LeadingIndicators.swift`, the feed's events) and the `DeepLink` a tap opens. The app's `NotificationScheduler` posts them each minute and `AppNotification` lists the same ids in the bell. They are local: a record from another device is announced once sync has brought it in, while the app runs.
  - Feature calculators: `SingleLine` (SLD), `InverterStation` (B2), `BuildingPanels` (B1), `Flood`, `SiteShapes`, `EquipmentCatalog` (bundled JSON).
  - Automatic drawings (`docs/specs/auto-drawing.md`): C1's pitch is `Roof.slope` (the frame's, or from the optional `ridgeHeight` over `clearHeight`; read the slope only through it), C2 and C3 draw `MeasuredRoofSection` from their measured sizes (`FieldKind.roofSection`), the tank's size and volume is `Tank.summary` (a round tank, `Tank.isRound` with `tankDiameter`, writes one span of D each way and `TankPoints.circle` is its boundary), and `TankPlan(details: true)` is the printed plan (beams, section marks 1–1 / 2–2, cross vents, the summary) that the PDF, Excel and DXF use. `parseSpans` reads "16@4".
  - `Sketch` / `DrawOp` vector drawing, shared by the screen and the PDF.

right of the plans the roof beam plan, `TankFraming`, after the team's `reference/Tank_Truss.dxf`: B1 on the perimeter and B2 on the interior grid lines, 0.20 m, a mark per span, bubbles, chains and the beam schedule, none for a round tank; the rows' section, front elevation, footings, plan modules and footing details are built from the team's components, one block each, `TankComponents` from `Resources/tank-components.json`, made by `tools/cad-details/components.py` from the reference DXFs; C1's PV export draws the walkways as the team's walkway detail, `RoofWalkway`, every n columns or one down the middle, `walkwayCentre`, which also runs them to the ridge, adds one along the ridge and, on a gable roof, the team's roof access top view at its left or right end (`TC_ROOF_ACCESS`, the roof page's `ทางขึ้นหลังคา`), lays the mounting grid over it (lower-case letters on the lines carrying L-feet, numbers up the slope, each foot tagged `b-3`, `RoofMountGrid`; DXF only, the PDF keeps the panel layout); the team's walkway detail sheet, `Resources/walkway-detail.json` from `tools/cad-details/convert.py`, is kept but no longer placed beside the plan) `InverterStationDXF` (B2's `ส่งออก DXF`, after the team's `reference/inverter-station` drawings: the top view on the B2 page's positions, the front view and the side view seen from the right end, outdoors the 75×75 columns on the grid with the mono-pitch roof 0.25 m lower at the back, indoors the existing wall, the rack with its posts at most 1.40 m apart, the AC tray at 0.50 m and the DC tray at 0.90 m, cabinets topped at 2.00 m and inverters at 2.10 m, the ladder and DC risers, the equipment schedule, notes and the base plate detail at 5 : 1, outdoors the shelter's member marks C1 / B1 / TB / R1 / P1 in both views and their member schedule, `InverterStationDXF.members`; the equipment, trays, ladder, columns, footings, door and scale figure are the team's component library's blocks, `TCR_…`, stretched to the equipment catalog's size, `StationComponents` from `Resources/station-components.json`, made by `tools/cad-details/station_components.py` from `reference/inverter-station/components/cad`) and `SiteDXF` (A2) call it; the real-scale ones are in metres (1 unit = 1 m) unless `millimetres: true`. Every file's text uses the style `CORDIA` (`cordia.shx`, width 0.85; Thai in TIS-620, `$DWGCODEPAGE` ANSI_874), its header carries the extents and its VPORT `*ACTIVE` view frames them; shapes are LWPOLYLINEs and marked symbols BLOCKs with INSERTs (the SLD's from `SingleLineDrawing.symbols`, D1's modules), so they edit as objects, PV modules (D1's blocks, C1's layout) sit on a frameless WIPEOUT that hides the lines under them (D1's plan module is the team's `PV_570W` block from `reference/PV_570W.dxf`, `TankDXF.moduleBlock`: one block `PV_<W>W`, its frame the module's length × cos(tilt) by its width, turned 90° where the slope runs up the plan), the plans' span chains and C1's section (drawn to scale for the DXF, `RoofSection(trueScale:)`) are DIMENSIONs whose measured text stays live (`RoofPlan.Layout.dimensions`, `TankPlan.dimensions`, `RoofSection.dimensions`), hatches and solid fills are HATCH entities, and every layer comes from the team's standard (`CADLayers`, the US National CAD Standard's `Discipline-Major-Minor-Status` names such as `P-FTNG-N` and `S-ROOF-OTLN-E`, E existing / N new, with colour, linetype, lineweight, plot flag and description; every object ByLayer, hatches on their layer's `HATC` layer, `CADLayers.standardsFile()` for a .dws; `$LTSCALE` from the A3 plot scale) (docs/specs/cad-export-dxf/SPEC.md, As built). - `PDFReport`: `ReportLayout` turns a `Job` into pages of `DrawOp`s (`ReportLayout+*.swift` per area); `PDFRenderer` draws them. The SLD page is A3.
- `ExcelExport`: fills `Resources/template.xlsx` by cell address (`ExcelExporter`, `template_meta.json`). Section slots are fixed (`ExcelExporter.slots`); extra sections get a copied sheet. `IECExcel` fills the PWA's own IEC 62446 workbook (`Resources/iec62446_template.xlsx`, byte-identical to `docs/specs/iec62446/1FORMI1.xlsx`) in place, cell by cell, so everything else stays as the customer's file; `IECExcelTests` checks the sample gives back the original. `MPWorkbook` reads a station's own `Master Plan Weekly` plan file into the MP plan and writes the plan and progress back into it in place; `SheetFormulas` then works out the sheet's formulas (IF, SUM, SUMIF, MINIFS, MAXIFS, WEEKNUM, DATE, shared formulas; anything else keeps Excel's saved result) and the S-curve chart's saved series, so viewers that don't recalculate show the new numbers. `MPWorkbookTests` checks it reproduces every saved result of the fixture. Read also gives the file's project facts (`MPWorkbook.Imported.setup`, `TCRoofCore/ProjectSetup`, `MPWorkbook+Setup`): the `แผนโครงการ` sheet's title (`แผนโครงการ P3-001 – <station> 163.02 kWp`) and set-up line (สาขา, ภาค, กฟภ. เขต, Inverter kW and พค.2, Zero export HV / LV, PPIM request, วิศวกร, Admin, ผรม., each part found by its own words), the installation points table, the Inverter station's type, and the plan sheet's `PROJECT` / `OWNER` / `รหัส` lines. An import takes them into the job (`Job.takeSetup`, `ProjectSetup.Target`: `projectName`, `capacityKW`, `customerName`, `inverterKW`, `StationDocs.Station`'s branch and its optional `code` / `area` / `peaZone` / `ppimRequest` / `zeroExport` / `admin`, and `RoutineReport.Settings`' `siteEngineer` / `subcontractor` / `employer` / `locations`): a matching import, the station's own template and a bulk import to its matched station fill only the fields the job leaves empty (`ProjectSetup.defaultFields`); the iPhone import sheet lists them with a tick each (`ProjectSetupList`), and the Excel menu's `ตั้งค่าโครงการจากไฟล์…` (`ProjectSetupSheet`) offers the kept file's facts to take over what was typed. A file of another station gives none. Export writes the job's facts back into the title, set-up, `PROJECT` and `รหัส` lines where they differ (`ProjectSetup(_:)`, `rewrite`), so a plan begun from another station's file names its own; the same facts give back the same bytes. The people are the job's: the set-up line is the file's word on them (`รหัส` gives only the code when it exists), export takes out of the lines whom the job has no one for, and the rows' `ผู้รับผิดชอบ` name the job's person for the row's role, or the role (`วศ.`, `Admin`, `ผรม.`) when it has none, while a row already naming the role stays (`MPTemplates.roles` from `wbs-roles.json`, made from the 20 cases by `tools/plan-template/wbs_roles.py`; `MPTemplates.plan(…, people:)` does the same for a moved plan's rows in the app; docs/specs/project-plan-wbs.md). A `Y` without a `เสร็จ` date comes in `undated` (`WBSTick.undated`) and goes back without one, so a station's own file, ticks and all, exports byte for byte (`WBSPeopleExcelTests`, all 102 files). `ProjectSetupTests` and `ProjectSetupExcelTests` (all 102 files) check it. Export also keeps the progress it wrote inside the file as a custom XML part (`MPWorkbook.Snapshot`, which Excel keeps on save); `รับเฉพาะความก้าวหน้า` compares the file with it (else with the kept original) and takes only what was changed in Excel since, a moved or cleared booking included, so what the app booked after the export stays, and caps each task at its weight; it also takes the WBS rows' `ผู้รับผิดชอบ` and `หลักฐาน` changed since the kept original, an emptied cell included (`MasterSchedule.mergingText`). Import runs the same check on each file (`MPWorkbook.FormulaCheck`): formulas it can't work out are reported in the confirmation, and a file where it works one out differently from Excel gets no results from the app on export (Excel recalculates on opening). `WeeklySlides` builds R3 (the weekly report) as .pptx for the meeting on `Resources/weekly_template.pptx` (a slide part per slide, pictures fitted from their JPEG / PNG size; `RoutineSummaryScreen`'s `PowerPoint`). `IECWord` builds the photo appendix as .docx (docs/specs/iec62446-word-appendix-swift.md, from the README's layout, since the customer's file isn't in the repo).
- `DriveSync`: Google Drive upload of report files and photos. Uploads go through a background URLSession (`DriveBackgroundTransfer`, set at launch by the app delegate through `TCRoofUI`'s `DriveBackground`; `DriveClient.backgroundTransfer`), so iOS finishes them while the app is in the background or after the system ends it, not after a force-quit. The body is written to `Application Support/DriveUploads` first and the token asked to last 30 min. `startUploadOnce` hands a file over and returns the wait: the job screen's `อัปโหลดขึ้น Google Drive` (`ProjectDriveUpload.run`) and `PhotoSync` hand every file over before waiting on any, with `BackgroundTime` (about 30 s) to finish that after the person leaves; a job whose upload didn't finish is kept in `drive.resumeJobs` and run again when sync starts (`resumeRequested`), and a file that landed while the app was closed is found on Drive by name and MD5 and recorded, not sent twice. Without a transfer (tests, macOS) uploads are sent directly. While the app is closed, `DriveBackgroundWork` (`TCRoof/Sync`) starts what is left: when the app goes to the background with a started job upload, photos or a plan workbook not on Drive, it submits an app refresh (`com.tcroof.internal.drive-refresh`) and a processing task that needs the network (`…drive-processing`, both in `AppInfo.plist` with `UIBackgroundModes` fetch / processing); the task loads the account's last role (`MemberRole.signedIn`, since `DriveRoute` depends on it), runs `ProjectDriveUpload.resumeNow` and `PhotoSync.uploadNow` on `AppStore.shared.container` (skipped while the store won't open), and asks again if something is left. iOS picks the time and runs none after a force-quit; try one from Xcode with the app in the background: `e -l objc -- (void)[[BGTaskScheduler sharedScheduler] _simulateLaunchForTaskWithIdentifier:@"com.tcroof.internal.drive-refresh"]`; in the simulator, `-driveBackgroundPass YES` runs the same pass at launch and prints whether work is left.

TCRoofCore depends on it for C1: `RoofMountYield` (TCRoofCore/RoofPV) runs it on the layout's faces in use, the standard's module matched to the catalog (`p570` = Znshine 570 W) and B2's inverter sizes (Growatt MAX 60 / 80 / 125), computed and never stored, with SolarModel as the fallback (no GPS, no hourly weather near the site, a module without a model) and the source said in the card; the PDF and the Excel print the card's figure (`RoofMount.printed`), and the PDF adds `พลังงานไฟฟ้าโดยประมาณ` after the layout (`roofEnergyUnit`: the P90–P50 range, the 25-year total, monthly bars, the loss chain, the source and the TH/EN disclaimer, `RoofMountYield+Report`).
