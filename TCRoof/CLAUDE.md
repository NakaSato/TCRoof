# App target (`TCRoof/`)

Read with the root CLAUDE.md. DEBUG launch arguments (`-screen`, `-homeTab`, `-install…`, `-monitoring…`, `-iec…`, `-survey…`, …) are in `docs/debug-flags.md`; UI tests in `docs/ui-tests.md`. Add a new flag or test there.

**App target (`TCRoof/`)** handles Google sign-in (`Auth/`), SwiftData models, Firestore sync (`Sync/CloudSync.swift`) and the non-survey screens. Firestore access is limited by `firestore.rules` (Firebase project `tcroof-1564c`): only accounts with a `members/<email>` document, and only the collections the app syncs, so a newly synced collection needs a rule. A manager (`users.manage`) edits that list in Settings › `สมาชิกทีม` (`TCRoof/Auth/MembersView.swift`): invite a Google account with a role and send the invite through the share sheet (with `config/public.installLink` when set), change a role, remove someone; the rules refuse a manager's own document. Sign-in checks the same list (`GoogleSession.signIn`): an account without a `members/<email>` document is signed out again (offline, only one this device has seen listed gets in), an account removed from it is signed out at the next sync (`MemberRole.onRemoved`), and when a different account signs in the previous one's records on the device are removed before sync starts if the new account is external (`LocalData`, which also forgets sync's known list so nothing is deleted in Firestore); a team account keeps them and takes over sync's known list (`CloudSync.carryKnown`). Settings' `สลับบัญชี` and `ออกจากระบบ` first wait for Firestore to confirm this account's writes (`CloudSync.flush`) and ask before going on without them, and Google sign-in uses a fresh browser session with the account picker, so on a shared iPad the next person isn't offered the previous one's account. Photos travel through Google Drive, not Firestore, without their GPS position and heading (`DriveClient` copies every image through `PhotoPrivacy.withoutLocation`, since the job folders are shared with contractors; the app keeps the location in the photo's stamp and its own file): survey photos by `SurveyModel.autoUploadPhotos` / `fetchMissingPhotos` (the Drive ids in the job JSON), and daily-report, ICL and IEC photos by `Sync/PhotoSync.swift` (a daily report's drawn signatures too: `PhotoAsset`s of the log tagged `signature.engineer` / `signature.inspector`, left out of `sortedPhotos` and printed on the DAILY REPORT's page 3), which uploads them into the job's `TCRoof Projects` folder and syncs one `photos` document per photo (its Drive id and owner) for the other devices to download. Every job folder is shared read-only with everyone who can open the job (the team, from the directory or `config/public.driveShareWith`, and the job's contractors and supervisor; `SurveyModel.shareJobFolder`, `DriveReaders`), after each upload and in each `PhotoSync` pass, each address once per folder, so every photo a report prints downloads on every member's device. The same pass carries a station's imported MP plan workbook (`Job.scheduleWorkbook`): it uploads to the job's `แผนงาน MP` folder, its Drive id rides on the job record (`scheduleWorkbookDriveID`), and the other devices download it so any of them can export the plan file. It hosts the survey module via `Views/Survey/` (`SolarSurveyEntry`, `PWASurveyLink`).

**Travel log** (`TCRoof/Travel/`): `Trip`, `Expense` and `ClaimMonth` belong to one person (`ownerEmail`); `CloudSync` listens to the signed-in person's only (`ownerEmail ==`), pushes them in their own batches and skips another account's left on a shared iPad (`OwnedRecord`); the rules let the owner write them and managers read them, never contractors. Receipts stay on the device until the month is submitted (`ClaimExport.upload`, the person's Drive `เบิกค่าใช้จ่าย/<ปี-เดือน>/<ชื่อ>/`), then their Drive ids travel on the records. A manager approves a submitted month or sends it back with a reason in `ตรวจใบเบิกของทีม` (`ClaimReviewScreen`, reading the person's records from Firestore while open): the decision is a `claimReviews` document (`TravelClaim.Review`) only managers write, never on their own claim, valid for that submission only; the claimant sees it (`ClaimReviews`, from `CloudSync`), is notified of a new one (`ClaimNotification`, a tap opens the month; also in the bell for 7 days) and can't withdraw an approved month. A submitted month's folder is shared read only with `config/app.claimShareWith`.

**Roles** (`docs/reference/roles/user-roles.json`, `TCRoofCore/UserRoles.swift`; `RoleTableTests` keeps the two the same): `members/<email>.role` is `manager`, `internal` or `external` (none is internal), read after sign-in by `MemberRole` and passed to views as `\.permissions`; check `permissions.can(.capability, on: job.access)` before showing an action. A job lists its assigned external people and supervising engineer (`Job.externals`, `supervisorEmail`, the job screen's `JobAccessCard`), and the team members working on it (`Job.teamMembers`, `job.assign_team`): that list opens nothing, but puts the job in their `งานของฉัน` (the PWA home's filter, `PWAOverviewHost.mine`, and the `แผนงาน` page's switch, `PWAPlanPhoneView.onlyMine`: one setting, `solarSurvey.onlyMine`, for both), lets only them, the supervisor and managers sign its daily reports (`daily.sign`, `Permissions.signsDaily`, the rules' `signsFor`; a job naming none of ours: any team member), and announces its feed news and its reminders (install days, the MP plan, the ERC flow, routine reports: `NotificationScheduler.heard`) only to them, the supervisor and managers when it names anyone (`Permissions.hearsNews`); the access card picks them from the team directory (`directory/<email>`, `TeamDirectory`: names and emails of the team and managers, no roles, read by the team only), which each team member joins when sync starts and a manager's สมาชิกทีม keeps in step; `CloudRecords` copies `externals` onto every child record and linked PWA survey. Scans and photos name who added them (`addedBy`, `AddedByRecord`, stamped at a record's first push from the device that made it): a contractor changes or deletes only their own, and one from before it names nobody and is the team's to change. An external device listens only to documents whose `externals` contains its email and pushes only a job's `iecReport` and its hand-over state `iecState` (`IECReport.LockState`): the rules let a contractor edit the test only while it is `open` and send it, never close or reopen it, and team devices drop a close the state doesn't record (`IECReport.trusting`). It also pushes the job's weekly requests R1 when the contractor changed them (`weekly.request`, `Job.weeklyRequestsUnsent`), signed `weeklyRequestsBy` with its own email (the rules check it); team devices drop a contractor's change to a week already sent or approved, or an approval they made (`RoutineReport.WeeklyRequest.trusting`). It no longer sends the MP plan's WBS ticks (CHA-71, docs/specs/project-plan-wbs.md step 2c): only the team ticks (`mp.wbs_tick`, `Permissions.mayTickWBS`; the rules refuse an external member's `wbsTicks` / `wbsTicksBy`, and team devices drop a write signed by a contractor, `MasterSchedule.WBSTick.trusting`). Each tick records its source (`WBSTick.source`: `team` ทีมติ๊ก, `daily` รายงานประจำวัน, `sheet` Google Sheet, `file` the plan file), shown as a chip on its row in `WBSChecklist`. A contractor picks the sub-tasks finished on a daily report line (`ProgressLine.wbsDone`, `DailyWBSPicks`), which wait as `รอยืนยัน` (`RoutineReport.wbsPending`, `Job.wbsPending`) until the team confirms them in the task's sheet (`ยืนยัน`, `Job.confirmWBS`) or acknowledges that report (`recordInspectorAck` confirms all its picks), or sends one back with a reason (`ส่งกลับ`, `ProgressLine.wbsReturned`); the Master Plan sheet's `WBS` tab writes `sheet` ticks. Team devices book every tick into the plan's progress (`MasterSchedule.bookWBS`, once per row in `Actual.fromWBS`). The contractor sends a request with `ส่งขออนุมัติ` (`sentAt`, `sentBy`), the team sends it back or approves it, and an approved one is read only until the team reopens it (`WeeklyRequestScreen`). The customers' phone and address also sync in `jobContacts` (team only); `config/public.contactsSplit = true`, set in the console once every device runs this version, drops them from the job documents. `config/public.minSyncVersion` stops older builds (`TCRoofCore/AppCompat`, the app's `AppGate`): a build whose `AppCompat.syncVersion` is lower shows `ต้องอัปเดตแอป` (with `config/public.installLink`) and `CloudSync` pushes nothing, its edits kept for the update; raise `syncVersion` in a change that needs every device on it before its rules or switch go live, and set the minimum once that build is out (`-appOutdated YES` shows the screen in DEBUG). The console's settings are two documents: `config/public` (every member: `minSyncVersion`, `installLink`, `contactsSplit`, `driveShareWith`) and `config/app` (the team only: `centerDriveFolder`, `claimShareWith`); sync reads `config/public`'s fields first and `config/app`'s older copies of them while it lacks one (`CloudSync.applyConfig`, CHA-59). `firestore.rules` enforces all of it; its tests are in `tools/firestore-rules-tests` (`npm install && npm test`; the emulator needs Java: `PATH=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk/bin:$PATH`). `-role manager|internal|external` (DEBUG) tries a role on the simulator; with `-screen` it applies at launch, to `-roleEmail` (`RoleUITests`). Drive uploads go by role (`TCRoof/Utilities/DriveRoute.swift`, its decisions in `TCRoofCore/DriveDestination`): a TC engineer with full Drive writes into the company's center folder `config/app.centerDriveFolder` (an id or link), each job as `1. เอกสารส่งมอบ/` (the PWA hand-over folders and file names, `TCRoofCore/HandoverFolders`, pinned to `project.json` by `HandoverFoldersTests`) and `2. เอกสารระหว่างก่อสร้าง/` (routine reports, IEC, ICL); a contractor writes to their own `TCRoof Projects/<job>/` and the app shares that folder read only with `config/public.driveShareWith`. Google access follows the role (`GoogleAuth.DriveAccess`, CHA-41): sign-in asks for the contractor's `drive.readonly` + `drive.file` (the team's shared photos read, nothing else in their Drive changed), and once the role is known `GoogleSession.checkDriveAccess` asks a TC engineer for full `drive` (`DriveAccessAlert` on the home, then `GoogleAuth.requestDrive` for the same account; `ภายหลัง` keeps their own Drive until the job screen's `อนุญาต Drive กลาง`, and `hasFullDrive` says which) and a contractor still holding full Drive from an older sign-in to narrow it, whenever sync confirms the role (`MemberRole.onConfirmed`). Another account picked on that page changes nothing.

**Monitoring reads Growatt from the device only** (`MonitoringStore`, `MonitoringFeed`): there is no server poll, and none of Growatt's figures go through Firebase. Only ตั้งค่า Monitoring syncs (`config/monitoring`), without tokens; each Growatt token stays in the Keychain of the device it was typed on (`MonitoringToken`). The Cloud Functions are the assistant's only. `archiveNightly`, `notifySupervisor` and `notifyContractor` stay in `Monitoring.Settings`, unread, so older app versions still decode the team's settings; alerts go only to the maintenance team on the iPad reading Growatt, so ตั้งค่า Monitoring shows that one recipient (CHA-94: the supervisor and contractor switches need a server path). At night (before 06:00 or from 18:30, `Monitoring.Daylight`, the forecast's day) an inverter out of contact behind a data logger that still reports is asleep (`Station.invertersAsleep`, set by `GrowattMap.station`): no alarm, no notification, not in the dock's badge, not counted against `พร้อมใช้งาน`, `พัก n` in the table; a logger silent past the threshold is still offline (CHA-110). `ต้องดำเนินการ` is one row per station (`Fleet.actions`, `Action.status` / `summary`), at the chart card's height; the chart's events are pills in a lane under the plot (`Chart.clusters`), `แยกสถานี` has at most six chips by station code (`Chart.name`: `MP 25`, else a plant name's leading code such as `P1_3`), the five largest and `อื่น ๆ`, and before sunrise the cards and chart show yesterday's figures and the forecast instead of zeros.
