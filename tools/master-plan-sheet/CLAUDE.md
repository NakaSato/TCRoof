# Master Plan sheet

The team's Google Sheet and its Apps Script. Tests: `node --test tools/master-plan-sheet/core.test.js`.

**Master Plan sheet** (`tools/master-plan-sheet`): the team's Google Sheet `Master_Plan_ประปา_เฟส3-4_ลิงก์แผนรายโครงการ` (a copy in `Project_Plan_TCRoof/1_Master Plan`) pulls from Firestore every 5 minutes through its own Apps Script, read only, with the IAM access of the account that turned it on (Cloud Datastore Viewer): `jobs`' `projectName`, `code`, `capacityKW`, `kind`, `stationDocs`' `code`, `scheduleInput` / `scheduleActual` (Swift's `JSONEncoder` dates: seconds since 2001) and `iecState`, and `installLogs` with a `report` (`jobID`, `date`, `engineerSignedAt`). Its menu's `เพิ่มสถานีที่ยังไม่มีในแอป…` writes: a new `jobs` document per unmatched station (`Core.newJobFields`: `Job.Record`'s required fields, the station code as `code`, `kind` `pwa`), undone by `ลบสถานีที่เพิ่มจากชีต…`; so a change to `Job.Record`'s required fields must change `newJobFields` too. Its `TCRoof` tab pairs each station row with a job (yellow override, station code, name, name + kWp) and gets the MP points per task, %, finish days, the IEC state and the daily reports; Mondays 08:00 it keeps `ประวัติรายสัปดาห์` / `ประวัติสรุป`. Its `WBS` tab lists every paired PWA station's tickable WBS rows with their tick and source; a `Y` (and date) typed there is written on the next pull into the job's `wbsTicks` as a `source: sheet` tick (`Core.wbsSheetChanges`, against the hidden `_wbs` snapshot; signed `wbsTicksBy` with the editor, who must be a team member; the installable `onWBSEdit` records who typed it), and clearing a `Y` takes back only a sheet tick (CHA-71). That write needs Cloud Datastore User. Every pull also rewrites a hidden `_app` tab listing every job in Firestore (`Core.appTabRows`): read it through the Google Drive connector (export as xlsx) to see what Firebase holds without a key in the session. Renaming or re-encoding those fields breaks it: `Core.js` is tested against `fixtures/plans.json`, the app's own encoding (regenerate it from a Swift test when `MasterSchedule.Input` / `Actual` change).

**Risk register for the app (CHA-81):** each pull also copies `ทะเบียนความเสี่ยง` into `config/risks` (`syncRisks`, `Core.riskRegister`): `{ json, updatedAt }`, `json` = `{ "risks": [...] }`, every row with an id in the sheet's order, written only when the text changed. A risk's `ขอบเขต` becomes the app jobs it touches (`Core.riskStations`: `ผรม. X` → the `Master Plan` tab's stations of contractor X, `สถานี > 200 kVA` → `pk2_required` `Y`, anything else → every station, no `jobs` key), through the `TCRoof` tab's pairing; a station with no paired job is left out. It needs Cloud Datastore User, like the WBS write; a failure shows in the pull's line and never stops the pull. The app reads it team only (`firestore.rules`), read only: edit risks in the sheet. Renaming its keys breaks the app's `RiskRegister.decode`.
